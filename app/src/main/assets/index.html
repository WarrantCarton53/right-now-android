Notifications

iPhone only lets App Store apps send alerts, so your alerts come from the Calendar app. Turn on “Alert” for any block you want a notification for.

  1. Tap Make calendar file, then open it and tap Add All.
  2. When you change your routine, delete the old Routine events in Calendar first, then make a new file.
  3. Add the Calendar “Up Next” widget to your home screen.

Your data

Your routine lives on this device only. Save a backup now and then, and use it to move your routine to another phone or tablet.
